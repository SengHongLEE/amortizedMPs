# Findings

- Earlier oracle-armed reflexes reduced aggregate success versus the canter baseline. V3 improved a subset of hard single gaps and hurdles with tuned action modulation, but did not improve overall across seeds or continuous gaps. Source: `MP_full_a1_jump_training_validation_2026-10-26.md`.
- Current 24-episode baseline at difficulty 0.75, 1.2 m/s, seed 123, forced canter: single gap 4/8, continuous gap 4/8, hurdle 2/8. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/reflex_dynamics_signal_baseline_24/`.
- Baseline trace shows a strong change in front-leg z-equilibrium action about 0.4-0.5 m before both gap variants; there is no comparably strong anticipatory change before hurdle. Hurdle-specific early-swing collision signal appears only close to the edge in a subset of failures. This observation is diagnostic, not yet a validated trigger.
- Dynamics can observe CPG phase, contact and force, equilibrium target/offset/velocity. The policy height map itself is outside the allowed reflex inputs.
- `EquilibriumPrimitiveDynamics` maps a normalized z action nonlinearly through `sqrt`: action -0.45 gives an equilibrium target near zero, not -2.25 cm. Offline detector analysis must use the actual target mapping.
- Gap signal using the FL z-equilibrium target falling edge appeared before the first gap in all 16 seed-123 traces at about 0.4-0.5 m; no such signal appeared on the eight flat traces. Hurdle horizontal foot-force contact occurred about 0.19-0.27 m before the base reached the near face.
- First isolated reflex probe, seed 123/difficulty 0.75/1.2 m/s, same 24 initial states: single gap 5/8 versus baseline 4/8; continuous gap 1/8 versus 4/8; hurdle 0/8 versus 2/8. The controller armed 24, launched 20, recorded flight in 16 and landing in 12. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_reflex_24/`.
- Hurdle failures show severe backward speed after front foot hits the riser. In two cases that baseline cleared, reflex front foot remained at hurdle-top height while forward speed changed from about 2.2 m/s to below -1 m/s. Reactive contact-only arming is too late for this trajectory.
- An anticipatory multi-state equilibrium pattern is visible around 0.4-0.5 m before the hurdle in both seed-123 and seed-321 baseline traces; it also has some late flat-terrain false positives. Candidate is under physical testing, not validated.
- A matched seed-123 hurdle-only probe at difficulty 0.75 and 1.2 m/s improved from 1/8 baseline to 6/8 with early equilibrium preparation; initial-state SHA matched exactly. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_hurdle_scaled_8/` versus `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_hurdle_matched_baseline_8/`.
- The same preparation threshold is not obstacle-specific. On the matched 24-episode seed-123 set, the controller achieved single gap 3/8, continuous gap 1/8, hurdle 5/8 versus baseline 4/8, 4/8, 2/8. Aggregate 9/24 versus 10/24. Early hurdle-pattern events on many gap trajectories explain part of the regression. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_scaled_24/`.
- Current MP_full_a1 actor already receives 60 height samples at x=0.15–1.25 m in a 12x5 grid; both actor and critic observation shapes can remain unchanged. The policy can express terrain-dependent preparation through existing equilibrium and timing actions; the reflex can consume only these filtered controller states. The old checkpoint was not trained to encode an explicit preparation signature, so this is a prospective training design rather than a current capability.
- In a matched seed-123 continuous-gap 16-episode probe, baseline was 10/16; gap reflex with hurdle preparation disabled was 8/16; delaying foot deployment by another 0.15 s was 7/16. Initial-state SHA matched across all three reports. Only 7/16 episodes armed under the gap detector; delaying deployment did not improve traversal. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gap_matched_baseline_16/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gap_no_hurdleprep_8/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gap_delay_16/`.
- The 7/16 gap coverage had a detector bug: disabled hurdle events were filtered after they consumed cooldown, suppressing later gap events. After moving filtering into the detector, gap coverage reached 16/16 but immediate launch reduced success to 4/16 versus 10/16 baseline. A 0.10 m velocity-integrated run-up restored 10/16; 0.22 m yielded 9/16 because contact-qualified launch could be delayed beyond the near gap edge. The old jump trajectory's extension was too strong for several successful baseline trajectories.
- With a gentler extension and 0.10 m run-up, a matched continuous-gap calibration probe gave 11/16 versus 10/16 baseline on seed 123 and 5/16 versus 3/16 on seed 321. This is narrow evidence, not sufficient for default integration.
- A dynamics-state hurdle discriminator uses front z-equilibrium target below 0.028 m and rising relative to a 0.10 s low-pass, while rear z target is at least 0.0295 m and not rising quickly. Baseline traces on seeds 123/321 showed this pattern on hurdles but not on gaps. A full paired physical probe with this detector, 0.10 m run-up and gentler extension: seed 123 single gap 5/8 vs 4/8, continuous gap 5/8 vs 4/8, hurdle 5/8 vs 2/8; seed 321 single gap 3/8 vs 3/8, continuous gap 3/8 vs 0/8, hurdle 5/8 vs 0/8. Initial-state SHAs matched within each seed. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_trend_full_seed123_24/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_trend_full_seed321_24/`.
- Flat controls on seeds 123 and 321 were 8/8 successful with zero arm/launch events on both seeds; matched baselines were also 8/8. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_trend_flat_seed123_8/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_trend_flat_seed321_8/`.
- Forced physical gait probes at difficulty 0.75 and seed 123 expose the remaining four-gait gap. Canter improved on all three first-band obstacles. Walk stayed 0/8 on each obstacle despite 19 recorded launches. Trot improved hurdle 0/8 to 2/8 but stayed 0/8 on both gaps. Bound improved hurdle 1/8 to 3/8 but stayed 0/8 on both gaps. Forced gaits are out of distribution for the five-gait source checkpoint, so these measurements do not prove an impossibility; they do disprove full four-gait validation at present. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gait{0,1,3}_seed123_{baseline,reflex}_24/`.
- A natural four-gait checkpoint (`1026_12-58-31.../model_6_70.pt`) selected trot in the matched test and produced no jump preparation event. Its reflex and baseline first-band results were identical (single and continuous gap 0/8, hurdle 2/8). This confirms current checkpoints do not generally provide a prepared signal without training.
- On a holdout seed 777 over difficulty 0.5 and 0.75, the canter reflex tied aggregate baseline at 22/48: single gap 8/16 versus 8/16, continuous gap 8/16 versus 7/16, hurdle 6/16 versus 7/16. The harder row had small gap gains, while the easier row lost one single-gap and one hurdle success. This is an explicit generalization failure for the claimed broad improvement. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_blind_seed777_{baseline,reflex}_48/`.
- A repeated seed-777 baseline with identical initial-state SHA but trace collection yielded hurdle 9/16 rather than 7/16. GPU PhysX results therefore have some run-to-run variance; matching initial states is necessary but not sufficient to establish a one-episode causal effect. Larger paired batches or repeated runs are required.
- The isolated four-gait training task completed one actual PPO iteration from a trimmed five-gait warmstart and saved a checkpoint, then a 12-environment rollout ran through 12 arms, 12 launches, 12 flights and 10 landings. The rollout succeeded on only 1/12 obstacles and selected bound throughout, so this is a pipeline smoke test rather than a performance result. Artifacts: `legged_gym/logs/A1/1026_21-27-52_A1MPfull-v3-18D-psF_3.333e+00-mpF_1.667e+01-MF_1e+03-noResume-dynamics-reflex4/` and `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_training_smoke_rollout_12/`.
- A 20-iteration, 256-environment four-gait PPO warmstart with canter-logit bias margin 3 still selected bound on every deterministic evaluation episode. On seed-777 difficulty 0.5/0.75, it achieved single gap 0/16, continuous gap 0/16, hurdle 6/16. This training recipe does not establish gap competence. Artifacts: `legged_gym/logs/A1/1026_21-35-18_A1MPfull-v3-18D-psF_3.333e+00-mpF_1.667e+01-MF_1e+03-noResume-dynamics-reflex4/` and `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_train20_seed777_48/`.
- Increasing support-foot push stroke from front/hind 0.05/0.07 m to 0.15/0.20 m, with unchanged reflex modulation and 0.10 m gap run-up, improved forced-trot first-band traversal at difficulty 0.75: seed 123 single 1/8 vs 0/8 baseline, continuous 4/8 vs 0/8; seed 321 single 0/8 vs 0/8, continuous 2/8 vs 0/8; seed 777 single 3/8 vs 0/8, continuous 2/8 vs 0/8. The short-stroke seed-123 reflex stayed 0/8 on both. Initial-state SHA matched within each seed. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_trot_{longstroke_seed123_16,gap_seed321_longstroke_16,gap_seed777_longstroke_16}/` and paired baselines.
- The same long-stroke profile on forced canter, seed 777/difficulty 0.75, gave flat 8/8 vs 8/8, single gap 4/8 vs 3/8, continuous gap 5/8 vs 3/8, hurdle 4/8 vs 2/8. Initial-state SHA matched. One of eight flat episodes falsely armed and launched late at global x about 7.24 m but did not fly; earlier flat seeds 123/321 had zero arm events. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_canter_seed777_{baseline,longstroke}_32/`.
- On seed 777, the same profile still gave forced walk 0/8 on both gaps and hurdle, and forced bound 0/8 on both gaps although hurdle reached 4/8. Walk continuous-gap landing global x averaged 4.92 m, short of the pit; trot reached 5.10 m and canter 5.29 m. The gait-specific horizontal range/landing remains a major limitation. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_{walk,bound}_seed777_longstroke_24/`.
- A focused walk probe with more vertical extension remained 0/8 on both gap types and only 11/16 flights. Delaying the gap run-up from 0.10 to 0.30 m also remained 0/8 and reduced launches to 2/16: by the later arming point, qualifying support was usually unavailable. The two 16-environment trial SHAs matched. This rejects two simple fixes for walk and points to coordinated phase/contact planning rather than a fixed extra delay. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_walk_seed777_longstroke_{extplus,runup30}_16/`.
- The updated objective requires at least one obstacle-capable gait, not that walk and bound jump. The source actor already has 60 forward height samples, but the reflex still only consumes equilibrium targets, gait frequency, contact force and body velocity.
- A 0.20 m fixed gap run-up for forced walk moved average continuous-gap touchdown to x≈5.03 m but did not produce a success (0/8); many trials hit the body after touchdown. Advancing deployment also stayed 0/8. Requiring two feet for landing recognition gave one single-gap success (1/8) but no continuous-gap success. These walk sweeps are no longer the critical gate under the updated objective.
- A 30-iteration, 256-environment joint-PPO trial with strong support stroke and initial canter bias margin 8 lost the source checkpoint's obstacle competence. At seed 2026, difficulty 0.5/0.75, forced canter yielded single gap 2/16, continuous gap 2/16, hurdle 7/16; the source modulation checkpoint under the same profile yielded 8/16, 13/16, 9/16. The joint-trained policy naturally selected bound on most flat/hurdle trajectories. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_train30_seed2026_{baseline,reflex}_64/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_train30_seed2026_forcecanter_48/`, `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_source_seed2026_forcecanter_48/`.
- Freezing modulation at zero learning rate in a second 30-iteration selector-only PPO trial preserved all 15 modulation-model tensors exactly relative to the source checkpoint. Yet the selector still favored bound. The dynamics-only hurdle pattern was indistinguishable across flat and hurdle when bound was selected; all 16 flat episodes falsely armed and flat success dropped from 16/16 no-reflex to 12/16 reflex. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_freezeselect30_seed2026_{baseline,reflex,trace}_64/`.
- The isolated detector now allows jump arming only for trot/canter, the two gaits with measured obstacle potential; other gait phases continue normally. With the frozen selector, this eliminated flat arms and restored flat success to 16/16, but obstacle success remained single gap 0/16, continuous gap 1/16, hurdle 3/16. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_freezeselect30_seed2026_gaitgate_64/`.
- A third 30-iteration selector-only PPO trial added a training-only reward for choosing canter when adjacent centerline height samples differ by at least 0.06 m. This uses the actor's existing height observations and does not feed terrain heights into the reflex. The reward remained negative over training and natural selection still chose bound on every hurdle episode. Paired seed-2026 first-band evaluation: reflex flat 16/16, single gap 1/16, continuous gap 0/16, hurdle 3/16; same-checkpoint no-reflex control 16/16, 0/16, 0/16, 4/16. Thus this reward does not establish a useful selector. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gaitreward30_seed2026_{reflex,baseline}_64/`.
- Actual policy-observation traces show why the first selector had difficulty: counterfactually flattening a gap height patch changed about 10–11 of 32 reservoir spikes and moved the canter-minus-bound logit difference by about 4, but flattening a 0.10–0.12 m hurdle changed less than one spike on average and moved that logit difference by under 0.1. Multiplying height input weights also changed flat outputs substantially, so simple gain is not selective. This is an offline inference on saved traces, not a physical success result. Artifact: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_gaitreward30_seed2026_obsprobe_24/`.
- The isolated selector now optionally reads the original 162-dimensional observation directly alongside reservoir spikes (`include_input_in_readout=True`). Five-to-four gait transfer zero-initializes all new direct-input columns; an actual source/expanded actor comparison produced exactly equal first-four logits on a recorded observation. A one-iteration simulator smoke completed.
- After 30 iterations with this direct readout, frozen modulation and the same sensor-based selection reward, natural selection still chose bound for all hurdle episodes and most gap approaches. Paired seed-2026 evaluation: reflex flat 16/16, single gap 2/16, continuous gap 0/16, hurdle 4/16; same-checkpoint no-reflex control 16/16, 1/16, 0/16, 4/16. This architecture alone did not satisfy the learned-gait or traversal gate. Artifacts: `legged_gym/logs/A1/1026_22-40-50_A1MPfull-v3-18D-psF_3.333e+00-mpF_1.667e+01-MF_1e+03-noResume-dynamics-reflex4-directreadout/` and `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_directreadout30_seed2026_{reflex,baseline}_64/`.
- A sensor-only evaluation rule over the existing actor height channels can choose canter before a forward edge, while the jump detector remains dynamics-only. With canter step length scale 1.5, seed-2026 first-band obstacle results were single gap 7/16, continuous gap 4/16, hurdle 9/16. The same checkpoint forced canter achieved 5/16, 5/16, 9/16. A shorter 1.05 m selection window achieved 5/16, 4/16, 6/16, showing that decision timing matters. Artifacts: `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_obswindow{105,125}_sourcecfg_seed2026_reflex_48/` and `docs/terrain/dynamics_jump_directreadout30_sourcecfg_seed2026_forcecanter_48/`.
- The source checkpoint's successful canter probe used a 1.5 gait step length scale, whereas the newer four-gait checkpoint defaults to 1.0. This configuration difference substantially changed gap outcomes; comparisons must match the gait scale, terrain set/order, seed and initial-state hash. The 48-episode paired reports above share initial-state SHA `e7f2e3c9e933a97c98c3f011c534575fb5934e787aab213059e2ce9fdc2211e2`.
- Sensor-rule distillation from a separate seed-2025 trace can make a true learned four-gait policy select canter from the unchanged 162-dimensional actor observation. A plain direct-readout model achieved only 8/28 hurdle-positive decisions on an independent seed-2026 trace and physically 7/16, 4/16, 7/16 on the three obstacle types. More samples and counterfactual height augmentation alone did not reliably fix hurdle recognition. - An opt-in signed centerline height-edge head at the readout made all 28/28 hurdle-positive decisions on the independent saved trace, without changing actor/critic observation shape or direct terrain input to the reflex. Its physical seed-2026 first-band outcomes were single gap 6/16, continuous gap 5/16, hurdle 9/16; flat control was 16/16 with zero reflex arms. The exact same-checkpoint no-reflex control achieved 6/16, 7/16, 7/16. Aggregate traversal tied at 20/48: better hurdle selection did not yet yield the required net crossing improvement. - A second opt-in architecture encodes the same 60 scan points with a small convolution before the fixed LIF reservoir, appending 16 internal channels while actor/critic observations remain 162/169. The existing LIF reservoir detaches its state, so offline encoder training required an explicit gradient path; normal inference/PPO still detach.
